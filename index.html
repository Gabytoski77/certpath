    Credentialing assistance

    $0

    Per fiscal year, as reported by approved CG COOL training vendors.

    Voucher first

    Don't register or pay for an exam on your own before ETQC approves your request.

    Any listed credential

    It doesn't have to match your rating, as long as it's on CG COOL. Missing? Email CGCOOL@USCG.MIL with the cert name and vendor site.

    Policy

    COMDTINST 1540.10, the Voluntary Credentialing Program.
    1. 01

      Find the credential

      Search CG COOL for the cert and confirm it's listed and approved for funding.

    2. 02

      Request CA in MyCG Ed

      Submit the credentialing assistance request for the exam and enter the full authorized amount.

    3. 03

      Wait for the voucher

      ETQC reviews the request. Hold off on scheduling until the approval and voucher come through.

    4. 04

      Take the exam

      Book with the testing vendor using the voucher, then sit the exam.

    5. 05

      Close it out

      Send your result or certificate the way ETQC instructs so the request is closed.
