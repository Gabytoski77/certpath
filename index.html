Privacy.

Short version: MyNextCert doesn't know who you are, and it doesn't want to.

What this site doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in, so there's nothing to log in to or leak.
  • No cookies, ads, tracking pixels or analytics scripts. The site's security policy only lets its own files run.
  • No forms that send anything anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your path answers, saved certs, exam date and settings are stored in this browser on this device only. They're never sent to a server. A backup is only made when you tap Save backup file or Copy as text in Settings, and it goes only where you put it.

To erase everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings (on an iPhone: Settings › Apps › Safari › Advanced › Website Data).

Hosting

The site's files come from GitHub Pages, delivered through Cloudflare. Like any website, they see your IP address and basic request details (browser type, page asked for) so they can deliver the page and block attacks, and they keep standard logs under their own privacy policies. The only numbers I see are Cloudflare's total visit counts, never who visited.

Links to other sites

Links to CompTIA, CG COOL, schools and other sites open their pages, and their own privacy policies apply there.

    Every cyber billet is coded with a DCWF work role (what the job is) and a proficiency level (how much the job expects). To hold the billet, you need a qualification listed for both. Start at 01 and work down.

    01 · What you need

    Three steps, on a clock.

    The clock starts when you're assigned to a cyber-coded billet.

    1. 01 · Within 9 monthsFoundational

      A cert, a degree or a training course listed for your work role and level.

    2. 02 · Within 12 monthsResidential

      Prove you can do the job at your unit: your command's on-the-job qualification.

    3. 03 · Every yearKeep it up

      At least 20 hours of learning a year to stay qualified.

    02 · The levels

    Basic, Intermediate, Advanced.

    Your billet lists one level. A qualification listed at a higher level also covers the lower levels, but only for that same work role.

    Basic

    Knows the fundamentals and does routine work in the role with guidance.

    Intermediate

    Works on their own and handles the role's everyday work.

    Advanced

    Leads, designs and checks others' work in the role.

    03 · The finder

    Find the cert for your billet.

    Know your billet's work role? Pick it to see every cert that meets it. Already holding a cert, or eyeing one? Pick the cert to see every role it counts for.

    04 · Not only certs

    A degree counts too.

    On matrix V3.0, a bachelor's in computer science, cybersecurity, data science, information systems, IT or software engineering meets Intermediate for every IT and Cybersecurity work role mapped here. A master's or doctorate in those fields meets Advanced, and so does NDU's M.S. in Strategic Information and Cyberspace Studies.

    Coast Guard

    Intermediate is the level the Cyberspace Qualification Insignia asks of enlisted members, so a finished bachelor's in one of those fields covers the foundational part. You still need your unit's residential qualification.

    Free courses

    DoD-owned training counts too. Warfighting Acquisition University (WarU) courses are listed for almost every role here, and DoD CIO points Components to WarU for low- or no-cost options.

    Hands-on range

    Hack The Box's Defense Operations Analyst cyber range is listed as training: 511 Cyber Defense Analyst at Intermediate, 531 Incident Responder at Advanced. TryHackMe isn't on the matrix.

    05 · Coast Guard

    Cyberspace Insignia checker.

    The Cyberspace Qualification Insignia is open to E-4 and above, chief warrant officers, officers and civilians serving in designated cyber-coded billets. Pick who you are, your billet's work role and what you already hold.

    06 · Matrix V3.0

    Already qualified? You stay qualified.

    DoD released Qualification Matrix V3.0 on Oct 7, 2026, and it changed which certs count for eight work roles. Under DoW CIO and Navy guidance, anyone already foundationally qualified under an earlier matrix stays qualified and doesn't have to requalify. Anyone coded to a work role and level on Oct 7 can finish the pathway they already started. Confirm your own status with your supervisor or cyber workforce manager.

    07 · The old labels

    Where did IAT go?

    IAT, IAM, IASAE and CSSP levels came from DoD 8570.01-M. That manual was cancelled on February 15, 2023, when DoD Manual 8140.03 replaced it. Instead of one cert for a broad category and level, every cyber position is now coded to DCWF work roles, and each role has its own Basic, Intermediate and Advanced options.

    “There is no direct comparison, or mapping, between 8570 IA functional categories and the DCWF work roles.”DoD CIO · 8570 to 8140 transition guide, Aug 2024

    Job posts

    Many still say “IAT II”. That usually meant Security+, but under 8140 what counts depends on the work role and level coded to the job, so ask for both.

    Certs you hold

    Certs earned under 8570 still count if they're current and listed for your work role and level. “Good for life” certs don't.

    Coast Guard

    COMDTINST 1500.2 builds Coast Guard cyber qualification on DoDM 8140.03 and the DCWF. The Cyberspace Qualification Insignia asks enlisted members (E-4 and up) for Intermediate in their assigned work role.

    The rollout

    1. Feb 15, 2023DoDM 8140.03 takes effect

      The 8570 manual, and with it IAT, IAM and IASAE, is cancelled.

    2. Feb 15, 2025Cybersecurity roles

      Foundational qualification due for the Cybersecurity element.

    3. Feb 15, 2026IT and the rest

      Foundational due for IT, Cyber Effects, Intel and Enabler roles; residential due for Cybersecurity.

    4. Feb 15, 2027 · nextResidential for everyone

      On-the-job qualification due for IT, Cyber Effects, Intel and Enabler roles.

    The Coast Guard has two separate pots of money for your education, and you can use both in the same year: Tuition Assistance (TA) for college classes and Credentialing Assistance (CA) through CG COOL for certs. Each pays up to $4,500 a fiscal year (Oct 1 to Sep 30).

    Tuition assistance

    $4,500

    Per member, per fiscal year. TA is paid straight to the school, for tuition only.

    Rate caps

    Up to $250 per semester hour, $166.67 per quarter hour or $16.67 per clock hour. Fees and books aren't covered.

    Who can use it

    Active duty and SELRES who are keeping up with unit and watch-station quals and have had good conduct for the 6 months before class. Enlisted: your last evaluation can't be marked Not Recommended.

    What it pays for

    Classes toward a certificate, an associate, a bachelor's or your first master's. Nothing past a master's. The school needs a DoD MOU (check dodmou.com).

    Still picking a school? Compare military rates, what's left after TA, and Coast Guard ties.

    1. 01

      Talk to your ESO

      Pick a school with a DoD MOU and a program. Pull your Joint Services Transcript (JST) first: your CG training may already count as college credit.

    2. 02

      Get a plan on file

      Certificate programs need the plan before the first class. For a degree, get an evaluated degree plan from the school early, or TA stops after your first couple of classes.

    3. 03

      Apply in MyCG Ed

      One application per class, 7 to 90 days before it starts. Class dates must match the school's term. Start a class without approval and you pay for it.

    4. 04

      Command approves

      Your CO or OIC approves, often through the ESO. Then ETQC authorizes it and the school is paid.

    5. 05

      Pass and close out

      Get a C or better (B for grad classes, Pass for pass/fail) or you repay the TA. Grades are due within 30 days of the class ending.

    Service you owe

    Enlisted must finish the class before the enlistment ends. Officers owe 2 years after the class.

    Dropping a class

    Tell your ESO and ETQC right away. If you repay a failed class, you can retake it once.

    Credits for less

    DANTES pays your first try at each CLEP and DSST exam. Pass one and you skip that class.

    Fees and books

    TA won't pay them. The CGMA Supplemental Education Grant can help with fees, books and study guides.

    Credentialing assistance

    $4,500

    Per fiscal year, separate from TA. First come, first served: once the year's money runs out, approvals stop until Oct 1.

    Pay, then get paid back

    CA is a reimbursement. After ETQC approves, you pay the fees yourself, then claim them back in MyCG Ed.

    What it covers

    Exam fees, renewal and maintenance fees, study materials and training courses for any credential listed on CG COOL. Voluntary certs only, not ones your CG job requires.

    Not listed?

    Email CGCOOL@USCG.MIL with the cert name and the vendor's site to ask for it to be added. There's no limit on how many requests you send.
    1. 01

      Find your credential

      Meet your ESO and search CG COOL. Check the cert's eligibility rules and when you could test.

    2. 02

      Request approval

      Apply in MyCG Ed at least 14 days before the exam or class. Exams and courses each need their own request. ETQC authorizes it after your command approves.

    3. 03

      Pay, then claim it

      Once it's authorized, pay the fees. Within 30 days, upload the receipt and a signed OF-1164 reimbursement form to MyCG Ed.

    4. 04

      Take the exam

      Register and test with the cert vendor. The cert's page on CG COOL has the testing info.

    5. 05

      Report the result

      Upload your result and certificate to MyCG Ed within 30 days of earning it, so ETQC can add it to your record.

      SkillBridge lets you spend up to your last 180 days of active duty training or working with a civilian employer while the Coast Guard still pays you. It's the bridge from your certs to a civilian job, so start planning about a year out.

      SkillBridge

      180

      Days at most, all inside your last 180 days of service, on full pay and benefits.

      Your status

      Permissive TDY, requested through Direct Access. Your command has to approve it.

      Leave

      Leave taken during the program shortens it day for day. You can use leave before you start if your command allows, and you can sell leave back, but there are no waivers to carry it over.

      Your date is locked

      Once approved, your separation date can't be moved or extended to finish a program.

      A rough timeline

      1. A year or more outStart TAP

        TAP has to start at least 365 days before you separate. Tell your command you want SkillBridge, and get your Work-Life transition manager involved.

      2. NextFind a program

        Search the DoD SkillBridge site for programs near you or remote. Pick one that fits the certs you hold, then apply with the employer.

      3. ThenGet it approved

        Request it in Direct Access. Enlisted attach a statement of service from P&A; officers attach the approved separation document. You also need an ethics brief within 12 months of your start.

      4. Before you startClear the paperwork

        Book your Separation Health Assessment early. Terminal leave and out-processing all fit inside the same 180 days.

      5. Your last 180 daysSkillBridge

        Train or work with the employer on permissive TDY.

      6. Separation dayYou're out

        Many programs end with a job offer, but it isn't guaranteed.

      Certs that help

      Most IT and cyber programs want Security+ at the least. Earn it, and whatever comes next on your path, while TA and CG COOL still pay.
